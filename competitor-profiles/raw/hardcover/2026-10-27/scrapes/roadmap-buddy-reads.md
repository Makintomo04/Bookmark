# Hardcover roadmap: Buddy Reads / Book Clubs

Source: https://roadmap.hardcover.app/feature-requests/posts/buddy-reads-book-clubs. Fetched 27 Oct 2026 with WebFetch (summarised by its model; quotes as returned).

- Status: **In progress** (Adam Fortuna, 29 Jul 2026). 105 votes.
- 2 Feb 2026: "Under consideration". 3 Jul 2026: "Planned", "currently in the early stages of planning this one!" 29 Jul 2026: "We're going to start an internal beta test of this in August."
- **Spoilers:** the original post (Tyler Sparr, 26 Sep 2024) proposes comments "marked as spoilers until your progress hits their same percentage". **Co-founder Ste replied (26 Sep 2024) that general discussions "only show up until the point you're read."** Follow-up suggestion: discussions time-locked and closable "whenever everyone finishes the book or by date".
- Polls: book of the month "via a poll" (Liam Jones, 3 Jun 2026); run-off poll proposal (Jacob, 29 Sep 2025, community).
- Private: "largely only be viewable by those you invite" (Tyler Sparr, 2024); "group / book club capability" for a small friend group (Jared, 1 Jul 2026).
- Progress visibility: "see everyones reading progress" (Liam Jones, 3 Jun 2026). No official statement on how progress will be shown.

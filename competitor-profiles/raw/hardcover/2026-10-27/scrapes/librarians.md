# Hardcover librarian model
Source: https://docs.hardcover.app/librarians/getting-started/ ("Last Updated 3 weeks ago")
Fetched: 2026-10-27 (in-app browser)
- Librarians: "trusted members... granted additional editing privileges. They are volunteer editors". Anyone can apply; reviewed by team; helps to have a month of activity and history of reports. Prerequisite: join Discord as confirmed member, link roles, fill application.
- Privileges: edit most book, edition, series, author fields; flag duplicates; split editions. High-impact actions (merging popular books, deleting) may need Senior Librarian approval.
- "Supporters inherit all of the editing powers enjoyed as a Librarian plus a profile badge, early-access features" (paid tier = edit access; matches pricing table "Librarian Edit Access").
- FAQ: all logged-in readers can fill empty fields and upload missing covers. Roles mentioned in reports: appender, librarian, senior librarian, staff librarian.
- Librarian Hub (report queue) in testing (Oct 2026). Blog "Become a Hardcover Librarian" 20/01/2026 by Brooke Manning.

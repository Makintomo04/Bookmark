## Pillars
- Find: Search and browse for new books – or find inspiration in other reader's libraries.
- Track: Track every book by want to read, currently reading, read and did not finish.
- Connect: Explore others reader's bookshelves and follow for their next reads.
- Discover: Uncover new books and authors based on your reading history and preferences.

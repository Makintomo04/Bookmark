# Hardcover FAQ
Source: https://hardcover.app/pages/faq
Fetched: 2026-10-27 (in-app browser; 39 <details> accordions expanded via DOM). No page date shown.

## Library / editions
- Book (work) separate from Editions (format Physical / Audiobook / Ebook, language). Choose edition per read via Book Button > Dates Read; Want to Read edition icon.
- Add book via ISBN/external ID -> searchable. Librarians & supporters can add manually ("hidden from search until verified by a librarian").
- "Inspired reads": when another reader saves a book after discovering it via your activity; notification.

## Imports
- Goodreads or StoryGraph CSV upload. Auto-retry on failure. Goodreads CSV only includes most recent finish date. Beta custom CSV import.

## Goals & stats
- Goal progress counts once Date Finished saved. Stats can take a few hours to refresh.

## Syncing & integrations
- "Currently, Hardcover does not support native syncing for e-Reader activity or reading progress"
- Kobo: NickelHardcover (community; progress sync, annotations as journal entries, status, reviews, ratings; by StrayRose); KOReader plugin (by Billiam); Calibre Web Automated Kobo sync.
- Kindle: "not currently a viable technical solution that allows direct Kindle sync."
- Other: Grimmory (self-hosted cataloguing), Readwise (third-party workflows).

## Roles & editing
- All logged-in readers can fill empty fields and upload missing covers. "Supporters and Librarians have expanded edit access, including the ability to edit existing book, edition, and author data across the site." Librarian application process.

## Account & privacy
- Profile flair (Supporter flair etc.). Referral link.
- Per-book privacy: Public / Following ("Visible only to readers that you follow") / Private. Lists and Goals same. Account-level defaults.
- Followers: anyone can follow unless blocked; remove follower. Mass-following may lead to ban.

## Data
- "Hardcover is ad-free. We do not sell user data, share it with advertisers..."
- No formal bug bounty; may offer Supporter upgrade.

## AI & ML
- "We do not and will never share user data with external services, including large language models (LLMs such as OpenAI)."
- AI-generated placeholder artwork previously used, since removed; "firm policy moving forward to not use AI-generated images or articles on the platform." "All artwork on Hardcover is human-made."
- Reading activity used in internal ML models for recommendations only.
- LLMs used in limited capacity for development (source code only; debugging, tests, boilerplate).

## Authors
- "Author claiming is not available yet."

## Supporters & referrals
- Supporter: Priority Support, edit access for book data, Librarian and Supporter Discord channels, Supporter flair, early access to new features.
- **"Every two successful referrals earns you one free Supporter month."** No limit.
- Cancel anytime.

## About
- "founded in 2021 by Adam Fortuna"; "very small team"; "independent and self-funded. We are not affiliated with any corporation or investment fund." "Hardcover is an open startup".
- Free: "is, and always will be, free to use."
- Ads: "does not use traditional advertising such as banner ads. In the future, Hardcover may support carefully designed book promotion that connects authors with readers"

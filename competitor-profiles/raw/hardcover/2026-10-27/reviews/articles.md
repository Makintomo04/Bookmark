## Hardcover public roadmap (first-party, user comments) — roadmap.hardcover.app
### Book discussions — https://roadmap.hardcover.app/feature-requests/posts/book-discussions
- 97 votes (among the highest seen). Requested by team 2021-10-03.
- Status: Under consideration (2022-03-28) -> In progress (2023-08-18) -> Planned, paused (2024-03-15) -> In progress again (2026-08-05, Adam Fortuna).
- Staff 2023: won't build until they have "an idea that readers love"; scope = per-book (also series/author/genre), conversations lasting years.
- Staff 2024: paused to build rich text editor, notifications, comments first.
- Staff 2025-08-28 (Emma): iteration planned after Hardcover becomes open source.
- Not addressed: groups, clubs, spoiler handling, per-chapter discussion.
- Users: Mike Cahoon 2026-02-11 wants Discord/Reddit-depth discussion, "could be huge for Hardcover!"; Yaniz Fuentes 2026-02-20 "would keep people on the app longer too!"; Vilandra 2023 wants forum layout.

## Editorial / comparison articles
- Book Riot, Arvyn Cerezo, 2025-02-12, "Goodbye, Goodreads: Five New Reading Tracker Apps to Try" — https://bookriot.com/new-reading-tracker-apps-to-try
  - Calls it "the book version of Letterbox HD" (sic, per users); devs "anti-Amazon", "pro-authors". Pros: private account option, unlimited books, sync. Cons: no reading timer, no Apple/Google sign-in. Paid tier is "more like community support".
